ABOUT THE PROJECT

A smarter approach to crop selection

The Smart Crop Recommender is a machine-learning web application designed to help users explore crop options based on soil and climate conditions.

Better-informed planting decisions

From growing conditions to crop suggestions

Different crops need different amounts of nutrients, moisture, and suitable environmental conditions. This project uses a trained Random Forest model to analyze seven input measurements and suggest a crop.

Nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium
Temperature and humidity
Soil pH and rainfall

Recommendations depend on the quality of the input data and the model's training dataset. They are intended as decision support, not a guarantee of crop yield.

HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps

Turn soil and climate measurements into a crop suggestion.

STEP 01

Enter your data

Provide soil nutrient levels, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall measurements.

STEP 02

Analyze conditions

The trained Random Forest model processes your measurements to identify a suitable crop class.

STEP 03

View your result

Review the suggested crop and revisit saved predictions from your account.

APPLICATION FEATURES

More than a crop prediction

Explore the tools that make this project useful and practical.

Machine Learning

A trained Random Forest model uses seven soil and climate measurements to recommend a crop.

Live Weather Option

The prediction form can retrieve available climate data for a selected city, with manual inputs as a fallback.

Prediction History

Signed-in users can revisit their saved crop predictions and review previous results.

User Accounts

Account login and protected pages keep each user's prediction history associated with their account.
